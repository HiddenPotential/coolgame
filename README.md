# 😡 Wacky Rage Golf

Three courses of pixel-art 3D mini golf, 18 holes each, plus three 9-hole holiday courses. Every single hole is out to get you.

Pick a course on the course map (🗺️):

- 🌳 **Meadow Links**: green grass, ponds, and a hand that lives in the cup.
- 🌵 **Dune Desert**: quicksand, cactus bumpers, camels crossing, vultures, sandstorms, mirage cups and sinkholes. The back nine adds a camel caravan, a zig-zag scorpion pass, twin sinkholes, a quicksand maze, dune buggies and a mirage market.
- 🌙 **Moon Base**: low gravity (lobs float, things roll further), black holes that suck your ball in, asteroids, the space void, and the UFO is home. The back nine adds a satellite slalom, the dark side (power cuts), binary black holes, an asteroid storm, a launch pad over the void and a space junkyard.
- 🎃 **Spooky Hollow** (Halloween): a moonlit graveyard with dead trees, glowing jack-o'-lanterns, a haunted house, bats and ghosts. Jack-o'-lantern bumpers, a witch's swamp, a haunted hallway where the lights go out, and a ghost cup that won't stay put.
- 🎄 **Frosty Village** (Christmas): snow and ice, so everything slides about twice as far. Snowy pines with fairy lights, snowmen, a gingerbread house, falling snow, present bumpers, a chimney with a flue that slams shut and candy canes on the move.
- 🐰 **Bunny Meadow** (Easter): a pastel spring with blossom trees, tulips, giant painted eggs, hopping bunnies and a rainbow. Egg bumpers, a cup that hops away, hidden-egg decoy cups and flowers that are not all friendly.

Each holiday course has its own music loop too.

On top of these, the desert and moon add **VAR review**, which watches your putt go in and then disallows it.

And on every hole, on every course, **the ground crumbles**. Weak spots are hidden all over the course and look exactly like normal ground.
Bits of the course crack and fall away on their own, sometimes the ground cracks as you roll over it, and if your item stops on a weak spot
(or near the cup, where it's most annoying) the ground starts shaking under it: you have about a second to hit it out of there.
Fall into a hole and it's +1 stroke, back to where you hit from. A wedge lob flies over holes. The tee and the cups never crumble.

On top of that, every hole has two more surprises, once each:
- **Teeter:** stop right next to the cup and the ball creeps up to the lip, wobbles with a drumroll... and rolls back out.
- **Sloped green:** stop close to the cup and the green turns out to slope back toward where you hit from.

More trolls can turn up on any hole:
- 🐕 **The dog:** stop near the cup and a golden retriever runs in, grabs the ball and drops it somewhere far away.
- 🚜 **Groundskeeper:** a ride-on mower drives straight across your shot and knocks the ball away (a moon rover on the moon).
- 🍌 **Banana peel:** one lands right in front of the rolling ball, and it spins out.
- 🦝 **Flag thief:** a raccoon steals the flag and turfs over the cup. It's invisible (even on the minimap) for two shots. Fake cups stay visible.
- ⚡ **Power cut:** the lights go out for a shot. You get a spotlight on the ball and a blinking light on the flag.
- 🪞 **Mirror mode:** left and right are flipped for one shot. The aim arrow shows where it will really go.

Even more trolls:
- 🌪️ **Tornado:** sucks the rolling ball up, spins it round and spits it out somewhere else.
- 🪨 **Boulder:** when your ball stops, a giant boulder comes rolling at it. Shoot before it gets there!
- 💻 **Fake crash:** the game "crashes" with an error screen (stop code: SKILL_ISSUE)… then says jk.
- 📉 **Inflation:** par changes mid-hole, usually downwards.

The plants are out to get you too (grass and trees on the meadow, dry scrub and cactus in the desert, alien moss on the moon):
- 🌾 **Rough:** patches of tall grass near the cup slow the ball and make it wander off line.
- 🌳 **Roots:** knobbly roots across the approach bump the ball sideways when it rolls over them.
- 🌱 **Sprouting weed:** a flower (or a baby cactus, or a space sprout) pops up right in front of the rolling ball, and it bounces off.
- 🪴 **Venus flytrap:** a giant flytrap grows next to the cup. Come near it slowly and it snaps the ball up, chews, and spits it out the wrong way (twice, then it naps).

Rough and roots move around every time a hole loads; the weed and the flytrap are part of the random troll pool.

Some holes also have gadgets:
- 🌀 **Portals:** roll into the orange one and pop out of the blue one (or the other way), same speed.
- 🛹 **Jump ramps:** hit one fast and the ball flies over water, quicksand and the void. Hit it slow and it just rolls over.

**Gary the Caddie** 🧢 introduces every hole with some deeply unhelpful advice, and at the end of a round there's an
**awards ceremony** based on what happened to you (Most Spaghettified, Dog's Best Friend, Indiana Golf, Thinking With Portals…).

No two rounds play the same: every time a hole loads it secretly adds a random extra troll or two (wind, the eagle,
a cup that moves, the hand, the mole, the lid, VAR, the UFO, or any of the new trolls above), and usually rolls a random condition, shown on the
intro card and next to the hole name: 🧊 icy green, 🍯 sticky green, 🏀 bouncy walls, 🫨 shaky hands, 🎲 random power,
🌬️ a steady breeze or 😵 hiccups. The intro hints whether something extra is hiding. It doesn't always tell the truth.

Slide past the cup and the crowd groans. The 🏳️ Give up button refuses to let you quit (and hops somewhere else),
and the score card counts how many times you tried.

**The course talks back.** A smug little face 😏 in the corner comments on everything: it brags about whatever it hid on each hole,
gets bored if you wait ("Any day now."), warns you off full-power swings ("Easy, tiger."), hands out backhanded compliments ("Lucky."),
mocks pinball shots, cackles at your penalties and trolls, rates your score when you finally sink it, and takes it personally
if you turn the music off ("Rude. I composed that."). It also does the taunting now.

Every troll has a limit, so every hole can be finished. There is no mercy rule: a hole doesn't end until the ball is in.
Each course has its own chiptune, made in the browser with Web Audio, that speeds up as your rage climbs and goes quiet while the ball teeters.
A rage meter tracks how it's going, and a commentator helps by telling you it's a skill issue (and gets meaner as the meter climbs).

You hit a real 3D golf ball with:

| Club | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Putter | Gentle and accurate, for short putts |
| 7 Iron | The solid all-rounder |
| Wedge | Pops it up over water and sand |
| Driver | Huge power, a little wild |

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step. The 3D view uses [three.js](https://threejs.org/) from a CDN, so you need an internet connection.

- Drag anywhere on the course to pull back and aim, then let go to swing.
- Keys `1`–`4` pick a club, `M` mutes everything, `N` (or 🎵) turns the music on and off, `Esc` cancels a shot, `C` (or 🎥) switches between the overview and a camera that follows the ball, `P` (or 👾) turns the pixel look on and off.
- Jump straight to a hole with `?course=moon&hole=5` (courses: `meadow`, `desert`, `moon`).
